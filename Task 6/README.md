# Task 6 – Product Review and Rating Management System

## Objective
Store customer feedback and ratings and use aggregate functions to identify highly rated products.

## Features
- Review and Rating tables.
- Customer-product relationships.
- Review retrieval.
- Average rating calculation.
- Identification of products with average rating of 4 or above.

## Files
- `review_rating.sql` – schema, review retrieval, average-rating, and highly-rated-product queries.
