# Task 1 – Requirement Specification

## Project
E-Commerce Order Management Database System

## Business Requirements
The system must manage customers, product categories, products, sellers, inventory, orders, payments, reviews, and ratings in a relational database.

## Functional Requirements
- Maintain customer records.
- Maintain product categories and product details.
- Maintain sellers and seller-specific inventory.
- Create and manage customer orders and order details.
- Record payment transactions and statuses.
- Store customer reviews and product ratings.
- Generate operational reports such as category-wise products, inventory status, payment analysis, order history, and average ratings.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Data integrity through primary and foreign keys.
- Validation for prices, stock, quantities, payment amounts, and ratings.
- Avoid unnecessary duplicate data through relational design.
- Support reliable querying and reporting.
- Protect customer and transaction information in an actual application implementation.

## Main Entities
Customer, Category, Product, Seller, Inventory, Orders, Order_Details, Payment, Review, Rating.

## Expected Outcome
A normalized database design that supports the complete e-commerce order lifecycle from customer and product management through ordering, payment, and feedback.
